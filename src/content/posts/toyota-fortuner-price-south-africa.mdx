## The 4x4 premium: about R100,000

Drivetrain is the Fortuner's big price lever. Across live GD-6 listings:

- **4x4 derivatives:** median asking **R619,000** (800+ listings)
- **4x2 and the rest of the range:** median asking **R519,950**

That ~19% premium buys the low-range transfer case that turns the Fortuner from a tall family car into a genuine bush vehicle — and because the 4x4s resell harder, a chunk of the premium comes back at trade-in time. If the vehicle will never leave tar, the 4x2 auto is the honest value pick.

## What else moves a Fortuner's price

- **The 2020 power bump** — 2.8 GD-6 cars from the 2020 update run 150kW/500Nm; the market prices the stronger engine in.
- **Seven-seat family duty** — interior condition matters more than on a bakkie; hard-used school-run cars discount visibly.
- **VX and GR-Sport spec** — the top trims carry real premiums, especially in the 2024+ model years.
- **Mileage bands** — under 100,000 km is the comfortable zone; the 200,000 km+ cars price on service history.

## Asking price is not selling price

Everything here is an **asking** price — where negotiation starts, aggregated daily across dealers and private sellers.
