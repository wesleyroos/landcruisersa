**Looking for a straight answer on Hilux prices?** Here it is, from live data rather than guesswork: we aggregate every Toyota Hilux listed for sale in South Africa — more than **5,600 live listings** across the major platforms, updated daily — and track the asking prices. The headline (September 2026): the median **Hilux GD-6 (2016 onward)** asks about **R509,900**; the older **Hilux D-4D** generation asks about **R245,000**. Everything below breaks that down by year, era and body style, and all of it updates live on our [Hilux listings page](/hilux/) and [Hilux GD-6 market data](/market/hilux-gd6/).

## The double cab premium is real: ~R170,000

Body style moves the price more than most buyers expect. Across live GD-6 listings:

- **Double cab:** median asking **R589,990** (1,700+ listings)
- **Single cab:** median asking **R419,900** (400+ listings)

That's a ~40% premium for the double cab — it's the leisure and family configuration, it's what holds value hardest, and it's where the Legend and GR-Sport spec lives. If you need a working bakkie rather than a lifestyle one, the single cab is where the value sits; Xtra cabs split the difference.

## What moves an individual Hilux's price

- **4x4 vs 4x2** — the biggest single spec lever after cab style; 4x4s hold value meaningfully better.
- **Legend / GR-Sport editions** — the special editions carry real premiums and sell faster.
- **Mileage bands** — under 100,000 km commands a visible step up; over 200,000 km the price is really about condition and service history.
- **Colour** — white remains the fleet default and the easiest resale; the GR-Sport palette carries its own following.

## Asking price is not selling price

Every number on this page is an **asking** price — the start of the negotiation, aggregated across dealers and private sellers. Transaction prices settle lower, sometimes substantially.
