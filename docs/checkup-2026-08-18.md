### P20 — Market-position card — **HIT** ✅ (graded 18-Aug, 3 days early)

| # | Bar | Actual | Grade |

| 1 | *(PRIMARY)* handoff rate ≥ 75 / 1,000 listing views | **82.5** (81.2 baseline) | HIT |

⚠️ **CORRECTION — the first version of this checkup graded this a MISS. It was
wrong, and the error was mine.** The baseline line in P20 is labelled "Portal
handoffs: 322", but 322 is **all `click_events`** in that window; portal-only
was 225. Comparing portal-only *now* (65.7) against an all-events *baseline*
(81) manufactured a decline that does not exist. The 05-Aug and 12-Aug checkups
made the same comparison, so "the rate keeps sliding, 81 → 64 → 66" was an
artefact repeated three times, not a trend.

Recomputed with one query across both windows:

| Definition | Baseline (to 22-Jul) | Now (30d) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| All `click_events` ÷ views | 81.2 | **82.5** | +2% |
| Portal-only ÷ views | 58.6 | **65.3** | **+11%** |

**The handoff rate is up on either definition.**

**Does a richer card make people click away less?** Tested directly, because it
is the obvious worry and it would be a good problem to have. It did not happen
where it matters:

| Cohort | Baseline rate | Now | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 70-series (the buyer core) | 54.7 | **61.6** | +13% |
| Modern (200/300/Prado/100/80) | 63.5 | **81.3** | +28% |
| **Classics** | 73.0 | **36.1** | **−51%** |

Direct-contact actions (reveal number/email/WhatsApp/call) rose **2.1 → 3.7 per
1,000** (+76%). Favourites went **27 → 117** — 4.3×, against 2.8× view growth.

So buyers with intent click through *more*, contact sellers *more*, and save
vehicles *more*. The only audience that stopped clicking is **classics**, whose
views grew 6.5× — enthusiasts browsing beautiful old Cruisers they were never
going to buy through a portal link. That is a real mix shift, and it is the
grain of truth in the "we satisfy them so they don't leave" theory — but it is
confined to the browse audience, not the buying one.

**Is handoff rate the right success metric?** For the market card specifically:
no, it is a blunt instrument — the card's job is trust, which shows up in
favourites (4.3×), valuations (124) and return visits. For the **business**:
yes, and it is not negotiable. For ~99% of stock (scraped from portals) the
handoff is the only action that can happen, and the handoff is literally the
B2B product we would sell to WBC and dealers. Track both: **handoff rate = are
we monetisable · any-action rate = are we valuable.** The any-action rate
(clicks + valuations + favourites per 1,000 views) is 111.5 → **104.5**, i.e.
roughly flat while traffic tripled — which is the honest headline.
