"click_events ÷ view_events", but the *baseline* line defines the metric as
**portal handoffs** (322 ÷ 3,966 = 81). Portal-only is therefore the correct
comparison and gives **65.7 — a MISS**. Counting *all* `click_events`
(incl. finance-calc opens, reveal-contact) gives 82.9, which would read as a
HIT. Same data, opposite verdict; recording it here so the 21-Aug grade can't
be quietly taken on the flattering definition.

climbs — 81 → 64 → 66. Read: the new traffic (classics, game-viewers, guides)
converts to portal handoffs at a lower rate than the old listing-led traffic.
That is a mix shift, not necessarily a card regression, and the card can't be
cleanly blamed or credited — which is exactly the limitation registered up
front in P20's "no A/B" caveat.
