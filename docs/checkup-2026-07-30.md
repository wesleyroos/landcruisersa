## Are AI citations dropping? No — it's small-sample noise.

There are **two different AI numbers** on the dashboard and they disagree, which
is the whole clue:

| Metric | Source | 30d | 7d vs prior |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI-referred** | Plausible (fuller sample) | 140 | ▼5% (flat) |
| **First-party LLM citations** | our `ai_referrals` table | 41 | ▼54% |

- The **−54%** everyone's reacting to is the first-party number going from **13
  visits last week to 6 this week**. That's 7 visits. The whole table has logged
  **50 rows ever** (since it went live 23 Jun). At 6–13/week you cannot read a
  trend off a week-over-week % — it's jitter, not a decline.
- The **reliable** number is Plausible's (it catches ~3× more AI visits than our
  own referrer capture). It's **flat at −5%**, i.e. steady.
- Week-by-week since launch the first-party series is **2 → 8 → 10 → 11 → 13 → 6** —
  that's *growth* then one noisy dip, not a downtrend.
- **Capture is healthy** — the newest `ai_referrals` row is from today 13:56
  (ChatGPT → a Prado listing). This is NOT a broken-beacon situation (the P10
  fear). ChatGPT is 132 of the 140 AI referrals; it's carrying the whole channel.
- Meanwhile **total traffic surged** (weekly visitors 909 → 1374). So AI held
  roughly steady in absolute terms while everything else grew — AI's *share*
  dipped, its volume didn't.

**Verdict:** citations are steady, not dropping. The scare is an artifact of
showing a "▼54% 7d vs prior" badge on a number that's only ~10/week.
**Recommended fix:** suppress that delta badge on the first-party card below a
volume floor (say <30/week), or widen it to a 30-day comparison — right now it
manufactures false alarms. (Not built yet — flag for a follow-up if you want it.)
